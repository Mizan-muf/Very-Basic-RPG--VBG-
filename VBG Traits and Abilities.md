# VBG-Z: SURVIVOR TRAITS & SPECIALIZATIONS COMPENDIUM

---

## 1. MAJOR TRAITS (+2 BONUS)
*Choose 1 at character creation. Represents your pre-collapse profession, military role, or primary archetype.*

### Tactical & Law Enforcement
1. **S.T.A.R.S. / Special Forces Operative:** Master of room clearing, target acquisition, and tactical firearm discipline under extreme pressure.
2. **Beat Cop / Law Enforcement:** Street smarts, standard police firearm handling, crowd control, and assessing suspicious behavior.
3. **Umbrella Security Service (U.S.S.) Cleaner:** Cold efficiency in bio-containment zones, tactical maneuvering, and hazard handling.
4. **Private Military Contractor (PMC):** Experienced with heavy weaponry, suppressive fire, defensive positioning, and combat communications.
5. **Close Protection Bodyguard:** Defensive reactions, intercepting incoming strikes meant for allies, and escort movement.

### Medical & Science
6. **Trauma Surgeon / ER Doctor:** Precision surgical procedures, stabilizing arterial bleeds, setting compound fractures, and managing medical tools.
7. **Paramedic / First Responder:** Rapid triage under chaos, CPR, field dressing, and carrying incapacitated casualties.
8. **Virologist / Bio-Researcher:** Identifying B.O.W. behavioral traits, understanding viral transmission vectors, and lab chemical synthesis.
9. **Pharmaceutical Chemist:** Formulating chemical reagents, synthesizing herbal compounds, and identifying toxins/antidotes.
10. **Forensic Pathologist:** Autopsy assessment, diagnosing time/cause of infection, and identifying anatomical weak points on mutated corpses.

### Engineering & Technical
11. **Combat Engineer / Sapper:** Fortifying structures, setting defensive traps, breaching reinforced doors, and structural integrity assessment.
12. **Master Mechanic:** Hotwiring vehicles, repairing combustion engines, generator maintenance, and jury-rigging mechanical parts.
13. **Industrial Electrician:** Rewiring substation panels, bypassing electronic keycard locks, and handling high-voltage hazards.
14. **Demolitions Specialist:** Safe handling of explosive ordnance, creating pipe bombs, and controlled structural demolition.
15. **Locksmith / Security Technician:** Bypassing mechanical locks, vault doors, security tumblers, and alarm systems quietly.

### Survivalist & Wilderness
16. **Wasteland Hunter / Tracker:** Stalking prey silently, tracking infected spoor, setting snare traps, and long-range marksmanship.
17. **Wilderness Survival Guide:** Foraging medicinal herbs, navigating unmapped terrain, fire starting, and shelter construction.
18. **Park Ranger / Forester:** Broad wilderness survival, heavy terrain traversal, axe/machete handling, and weather assessment.
19. **Deep Woods Outfitter:** Resource budgeting, pack optimization, and heavy load carrying over long distances.

### Urban & Underworld
20. **Urban Scavenger:** Spotting hidden supply caches, navigating ruined building crawlspaces, and assessing structural collapse risks.
21. **Burglar / Second-Story Thief:** Silent rooftop traversal, climbing drainage pipes, breaking and entering, and moving undetected.
22. **Black Market Fixer:** Bartering, assessing item value, finding contraband caches, and reading human intentions.
23. **Street Brawler / Enforcer:** Brutal blunt force melee, using heavy improvised weapons, shoving through crowds, and street toughness.
24. **Stunt Driver / Courier:** High-speed vehicle handling, navigating road blockades, and defensive vehicular maneuvering.

## 2. MINOR TRAITS (+1 BONUS)
*Choose 1 at character creation. Represents specific habits, tactical techniques, or secondary background skills.*

### Combat & Physical
1. **Steady Aim:** +1 to firearm accuracy when bracing against cover or taking time to aim.
2. **Quick Draw:** +1 when reacting to sudden ambushes or drawing weapons in close quarters.
3. **Double Tap:** +1 to followup handgun shots on a target that was just hit.
4. **Heavy Swinger:** +1 to Force rolls when using two-handed blunt weapons (fireaxe, sledgehammer).
5. **Knife Fighter:** +1 when using combat knives or blades in close-quarters defense.
6. **Point Blank Reflex:** +1 to shotgun blasts against targets within reach.
7. **Iron Grip:** +1 when resisting being disarmed or holding a barricade shut against a mob.
8. **Fleet Footed:** +1 to sprint speed and leaping across rooftops or broken stairwells.
9. **Hard Hitter:** +1 to unarmed shoves and kicks against charging shamblers.
10. **Suppressive Instinct:** +1 to suppressive rifle fire keeping hostiles pinned.

### Stealth & Awareness
11. **Silent Step:** +1 to stealth rolls moving through glass, debris, or cluttered hallways.
12. **Light Sleeper:** +1 to passive awareness while resting; impossible to catch completely unprepared.
13. **Sixth Sense:** +1 to spotting hidden ambushers (Lickers on ceilings, corpses playing dead).
14. **Shadow Walker:** +1 when using darkness and visual cover to bypass infected lines of sight.
15. **Threat Recognition:** +1 to assessing how dangerous an unfamiliar mutation is before engaging.
16. **Echo Locator:** +1 to tracking sounds through walls, ventilation ducts, and floorboards.

### Medical & Resilience
17. **Field Chemist:** +1 when synthesizing herbal sprays or chemical mixtures.
18. **Tourniquet Specialist:** +1 when rapidly treating the Bleeding condition.
19. **Iron Stomach:** +1 to resisting food poisoning or illness from stale rations and unpurified water.
20. **Cold-Blooded:** +1 to Focus checks when resisting horror jump-scares, gore shock, and panic.
21. **Pain Tolerance:** +1 to remaining functional while carrying traumatic conditions.
22. **Adrenaline Spike:** +1 to your next action roll immediately after soaking a hit.

### Technical & Scavenging
23. **Scrap Gunsmith:** +1 to maintaining weapon durability and clearing firearm jams.
24. **Hoarder's Eye:** +1 to finding concealed ammo boxes or hidden key items in ransacked rooms.
25. **Jury-Rigger:** +1 to making temporary repairs on broken tools using scrap metal and duct tape.
26. **Lockpick Finesse:** +1 when picking mechanical tumbler locks with hairpin tools.
27. **Hotwire Touch:** +1 when starting vehicles or powering generators without the original keys.
28. **Pack Optimizer:** +1 when organizing gear (allows stacking 1 extra small item per slot).
29. **Blueprint Reader:** +1 to navigating unfamiliar facilities using discovered building maps.
30. **Electronic Bypass:** +1 to rerouting circuit breaker panels and security consoles.

## 3. TACTICAL SPECIALIZATIONS (DOMAINS)
*Choose 1 Specialization Domain. Abilities cost Strain to perform exceptional tactical feats.*

### 1. Close-Quarters Defense (CQC)
- **Minor (0 Strain):** Swift defensive shove or sweep to create breathing room from a basic shambler.
- **Standard (1 Strain):** Defensive knife counter-strike during a grab to break free without taking damage.
- **Major (2 Strain):** Heavy tackle/throw that knocks a primary target into a cluster, staggering multiple enemies.

### 2. Precision Marksmanship
- **Minor (0 Strain):** Controlled double-tap on a stationary target to maximize stopping power.
- **Standard (1 Strain):** Called shot on a B.O.W. weak point (eyeball, exposed brain, knee joint) to bypass 1 Armor box.
- **Major (2 Strain):** Impossible trick shot under fire (severing an environmental crane cable, hitting a distant gas tank).

### 3. Field Medicine & Triage
- **Minor (0 Strain):** Rapid visual diagnostic to identify an ally's exact infection stage or wound severity.
- **Standard (1 Strain):** Combat suture to clear the *Bleeding* condition during active combat in a single action.
- **Major (2 Strain):** Emergency adrenaline stabilization to bring a Downed/Bleeding Out ally back to conscious state (1 Strain).

### 4. Structural Fortification & Traps
- **Minor (0 Strain):** Quick-wedge a wooden/metal door or assess the weakest structural point in a room.
- **Standard (1 Strain):** Rapid barricade reinforcement that absorbs 2 hits before hostiles can breach.
- **Major (2 Strain):** Jury-rig an environmental trap (electrified puddle, gas tank tripwire) that engulfs a doorway.

### 5. Infiltration & Evasion
- **Minor (0 Strain):** Ghost step to slip across an open doorway unspotted by wandering shamblers.
- **Standard (1 Strain):** Combat roll / evasive duck that completely evades an incoming lunging strike or pounce.
- **Major (2 Strain):** Flash/distraction maneuver that breaks line of sight for the entire party, allowing a clean retreat.

### 6. Bio-Chemical Warfare
- **Minor (0 Strain):** Identify the specific virus strain (T-Virus, G-Virus, Plagas) and standard behavioral triggers.
- **Standard (1 Strain):** Formulate a volatile acid or incendiary vial from laboratory chemicals.
- **Major (2 Strain):** Synthesize an emergency viral suppressant that temporarily halts an infected ally's progression for 24 hours.

### 7. Heavy Weapon Demolitions
- **Minor (0 Strain):** Calculate the exact blast radius and structural impact of an explosive before detonating.
- **Standard (1 Strain):** Direct a shotgun blast or grenade cone to clear a path through a barricaded choke point.
- **Major (2 Strain):** Point-blank blast detonation designed to stagger a boss-tier Stalker/Tyrant and force a retreat.
