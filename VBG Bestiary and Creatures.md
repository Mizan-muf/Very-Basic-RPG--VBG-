## 1. B.O.W. DESIGN BLUEPRINT (TEMPLATE)

Use this template to design balanced, asynchronous threats for VBG-Z. Remember: **Monsters never roll dice**. They declare incoming threats that force players to react using their Major + Minor action economy.

```text
==================================================
B.O.W. NAME / DESIGNATION
==================================================
Threat Tier: [Minor | Standard | Major | Boss]
Armor: [⬜ (1 Box) to ⬜⬜⬜⬜⬜ (5 Boxes)]
Noise Sensitivity: [Acoustic Blind | Aggressive +1 | Stalker 6/6 Spawn]

DESCRIPTION & PRESENCE:
[Brief sensory description: sight, sound, smell, movement posture.]

INCOMING THREATS (Actions declared by GM):
• [Threat 1 - Standard Attack]: Forces [Stat] TN [10/15/20]. Failure: [Damage/Condition].
• [Threat 2 - Grab / Secondary]: Forces [Stat] TN [10/15/20]. Failure: [Bite / Infection +1 / Prone].
• [Threat 3 - Enrage / Environmental]: Special movement, wall climb, or AoE shockwave.

STRENGTHS & BEHAVIOR:
• [Strengths / Resistances]: (e.g., Immune to pain, rapid regeneration, ceiling climber).
• [Tactics]: (e.g., Lurks in vents, rushes sound of unsuppressed gunfire).

WEAKNESSES & EXPLOITS:
• [Weaknesses]: (e.g., Called shot headshots TN 20, fire vulnerability, blind to walking pace).
• [V-ACT / Death State]: Does it mutate into a Crimson Head if not incinerated?
==================================================
```

## 2. STARTER ENEMY STAT BLOCKS (READY TO RUN)

### A. MINOR THREATS (Swarms & Roamers)

#### 1. Urban Shambler (Solitary Wanderer)
* **Threat Tier:** Minor
* **Armor:** ⬜ (1 Box)
* **Noise Sensitivity:** Drifts toward any sound (+1 Noise brings 1 shambler into adjacent corridors).
* **Description:** A freshly turned civilian or police officer. Sluggish gait, vacant milky eyes, persistent guttural groan.
* **Incoming Threats:**
  * *Lunging Grab:* Forces Finesse or Force TN 12. Failure: Inflicts 1 Strain and sets up an immediate bite next turn unless broken.
  * *Bite:* If a survivor starts their turn already grabbed, forces Force TN 15 to break free. Failure: Inflicts 1 Strain, causes the **Bleeding** condition, and advances the **Virus Infection Track by +1 Stage**. *(Can be countered instantly by expending 1 Knife Durability box or firing 1 Handgun shot).*
* **Strengths:** Ignores non-lethal body trauma; unphased by darkness.
* **Weaknesses:** Headshots (Called Shot TN 18 destroys instantly).
* **V-ACT Risk:** High. If corpse is left intact without burning or decapitation, revives as a Crimson Head after 2 scenes.

#### 2. Infected K-9 / Cerberus (Zombie Dog)
* **Threat Tier:** Minor (Deadly in packs of 2–3)
* **Armor:** ⬜ (1 Box)
* **Noise Sensitivity:** Rushes the exact location of gunfire within 1 turn.
* **Description:** Emaciated, skin-sloughed Doberman or police dog. Incredibly fast, predatory snarls, bounding gait.
* **Incoming Threats:**
  * *Sprinting Pounce:* Forces Finesse TN 15. Failure: Knocks survivor prone and inflicts 1 Armor/Strain damage.
  * *Throat Tear:* Forces Force TN 15 to kick off. Failure: Inflicts 1 Strain and causes the **Bleeding** condition.
* **Strengths:** High movement speed (can close distant zones in a single action); cannot be outrun on foot without barricading doors.
* **Weaknesses:** Frail skeletal structure. 1 clean Shotgun blast or solid blunt force hit kills instantly.
* **V-ACT Risk:** None.

### B. STANDARD THREATS (Party-Level Hazards)

#### 3. Shambler Horde (Swarm Cluster)
* **Threat Tier:** Standard
* **Armor:** ⬜⬜⬜ (3 Boxes total for the cluster)
* **Noise Sensitivity:** Triggered at Noise Clock 4/6 or higher.
* **Description:** A tight mass of 4–6 rotting infected pressing through narrow hallways, doorways, or broken shopfronts.
* **Incoming Threats:**
  * *Overwhelming Crush:* Forces Force TN 15. Failure: Pinned against wall/floor, marks 1 Armor/Strain box.
  * *Feeding Frenzy:* If pinned, forces Force TN 18. Failure: 2 Strain, **Bleeding**, and **+1 Infection Stage**.
* **Strengths:** Immune to single-target small arms knockdown.
* **Weaknesses:** Fire & Area Explosives (Grenades/Molotovs wipe the entire horde in 1 blast). Shotguns hit all 3 boxes if fired at point-blank range.

#### 4. Crimson Head (V-ACT Mutant)
* **Threat Tier:** Standard
* **Armor:** ⬜⬜ (2 Boxes)
* **Noise Sensitivity:** Hyper-aggressive; pursues fleeing survivors across sector transitions.
* **Description:** A revived shambler with crimson skin, elongated razor claws, heightened muscle mass, and rapid, twitching sprint mechanics.
* **Incoming Threats:**
  * *Frenzied Claw Slash:* Forces Finesse TN 16. Failure: Inflicts 1 Armor/Strain damage and causes **Bleeding**.
  * *Leaping Decapitation Strike:* Forces Finesse TN 18. Failure: Inflicts 2 Strain and the **Limb Damaged** condition.
* **Strengths:** Sprints at full human speed; attacks twice during combat resolution if unengaged.
* **Weaknesses:** Shotgun blasts to the chest or head deal double damage. Incendiary damage destroys instantly.

### C. MAJOR THREATS (Elite B.O.W.s & Set Pieces)

#### 5. Licker (Acoustic Apex Predator)
* **Threat Tier:** Major
* **Armor:** ⬜⬜⬜ (3 Boxes)
* **Noise Sensitivity:** **Acoustic Hunter.** Detects running, gunfire (+1/+2 Noise), or loud interactions instantly.
* **Description:** Quadrupedal mutant with exposed brain matter, skinless muscle tissue, razor-sharp scythe talons, and a 10-foot prehensile barbed tongue. Climbs walls and hangs from ceiling rafters.
* **Incoming Threats:**
  * *Ceiling Drop / Talon Flurry:* Forces Finesse TN 18. Failure: 1 Armor/Strain damage and inflicts **Limb Damaged (Leg)**.
  * *Barbed Tongue Harpoon:* Ranged strike. Forces Finesse TN 16. Failure: 1 Strain, **Bleeding**, and pulls the survivor across the room into melee range.
* **Strengths:** Extreme vertical mobility (ignores ground barricades); immune to visual flashbangs.
* **Weaknesses:** **Completely Blind.** Moving at a slow walking pace requires only Finesse TN 10 to walk right underneath it without alerting it. Highly vulnerable to 12-Gauge Shotgun blasts and Acid rounds.

#### 6. Drain Deimos (Infestation Crawler)
* **Threat Tier:** Major
* **Armor:** ⬜⬜ (2 Boxes)
* **Noise Sensitivity:** Nests in sewer grates, ventilation shafts, and electrical substations.
* **Description:** Giant mutated flea/arthropod that scuttles across walls and ceilings. Emits a high-pitched chittering hiss.
* **Incoming Threats:**
  * *Ceiling Pounce / Parasite Implantation:* Forces Finesse TN 16. Failure: Inflicts 1 Strain and inflicts the **Poisoned** condition (+1 Infection Stage).
  * *Acid Bile Spit:* Ranged cone. Forces Finesse TN 15. Failure: Instantly dissolves 1 Armor Box on worn vests.
* **Strengths:** Wall crawler; swarms from dark ventilation ducts.
* **Weaknesses:** Vulnerable to fire and electric hazards (car batteries, exposed wiring).

### D. BOSS-TIER THREAT (The Roaming Nemesis)

#### 7. The Pursuer / Nemesis-Type Tyrant
* **Threat Tier:** Boss
* **Armor:** ⬜⬜⬜⬜⬜ (5 Boxes; regenerates 1 Armor Box at the start of each scene if not burning).
* **Noise Sensitivity:** **Spawns on 6/6 Breach Events.** Smashes through walls directly toward the party's location.
* **Description:** A 9-foot bio-engineered super-soldier encased in black heavy restraint leather. Audible heavy combat boot thuds precede its entrance. Cold, intelligent, and relentless.
* **Incoming Threats:**
  * *Choke Lift & Wall Slam:* Forces Force TN 20. Failure: 2 Strain, **Panicked** condition, and smashes the survivor through a wooden door/furniture.
  * *Tentacle Pierce:* Ranged strike. Forces Finesse TN 18. Failure: 1 Strain, **Bleeding**, and **+1 Infection Stage**.
  * *Unstoppable Advance:* Smashes environmental barricades as a free action; cannot be locked out of rooms.
* **Strengths:** Cannot be permanently killed with small arms. Regenerates armor.
* **Weaknesses:**
  * Concentrated heavy fire (Magnum / Shotgun / Explosives) can break its 5 Armor boxes, forcing it to drop to one knee and enter **Stunned Stasis for 1 Scene**, allowing the party to escape.
  * Environmental Traps (dropping electrical cranes, fuel tanker explosions) instantly bypass its armor.
