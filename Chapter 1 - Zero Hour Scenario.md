## 3. DETAILED SCENE BREAKDOWN

### SCENE 1: THE CRACK IN THE GLASS (Atmosphere & Introduction)
* **Location:** Central Atrium, Ground Floor (Fountain & Directory Kiosks).
* **Atmosphere:** Fluorescent lights hum with slight electrical flickering. Stores are prematurely pulling down security grates. Shoppers carry heavy emergency grocery bags. Outside, muffled sirens echo from the **Arklay Forest perimeter (North/Northwest)** and down **Central Street**.
* **Intercom Announcement 1 (19:50 EST):**
  > *"Attention valued shoppers. By order of the Raccoon City Police Department and Mall Security, please remain vigilant of your surroundings. If you observe individuals exhibiting extreme agitation, disorientation, or flu-like symptoms, please do not engage. Report immediately to the nearest security kiosk on Level 1."*
* **Player Hook:** Each player establishes why they are here (buying emergency batteries/rations, working an evening retail/security shift, meeting an acquaintance, or taking shelter from the pouring rain).

---

### SCENE 2: THE EAST PARKING STRUCTURE B STANDOFF (The First Sighting)
* **Location:** East Wing Exit (Glass doors leading to Multi-Story Parking Structure B, facing the **City Hall Plaza**).
* **The Situation:**
  * Two senior Mall Security guards in yellow-and-black jackets are struggling to padlock the heavy glass crash doors shut.
  * Outside in the rain, a dozen pale-faced, disheveled citizens are pounding against the reinforced glass with bare fists, smearing bloody handprints. Their movements are jerky and uncoordinated.
  * A line of 30+ frustrated shoppers is blocked from reaching their parked cars, yelling at the guards.
* **The Rookie's Wound (NPC Encounter):**
  * Slumped on a nearby bench is rookie security guard **Officer Kevin Vance** (21 years old), clutching a blood-soaked towel to his right shoulder.
  * A civilian pharmacist, **Arthur Pendelton**, is nervously trying to bandage it with gauze.
  * *Investigation / Medicine Note:* A Focus TN 10 check confirms it is a **jagged metal turnstile laceration** from a shattered gate, *not* a bite or claw mark. Vance is pale and in shock, mumbling: *"They weren't stopping... one guy just kept pushing his face against the turnstile spike... he didn't even scream..."*

---

### SCENE 3: EXPLORATION & THE 20-MINUTE SCAVENGE WINDOW
* **Parameters:** The party has a brief window before total lockdown. Each player can visit **one** wing or store to make an initial investigation or Focus check.
* **Exploration Locations & Scavenge Opportunities:**
  1. **"Grizzly Outdoors" (Sports & Camping Store - 2nd Floor):**
     * Metal grates half-closed.
     * *Focus TN 10 Scavenge:* Yields a **Hunting Knife (1 Slot, ⬜⬜⬜ Durability)** or **Heavy Crowbar (2 Slots, ⬜⬜⬜ Durability, +1 Breaching)**.
  2. **"Pharm-Plus Express" (1st Floor East):**
     * Ransacked shelves, medicine cabinets locked behind glass.
     * *Finesse / Focus TN 12:* Yields **1 Green Herb (1/4 Slot)** or **1 Basic Bandage Pack (Stops Bleeding)**.
  3. **"Tech Nexus / RadioShack" (Electronics - 1st Floor West):**
     * Bank of demo TVs tuned to regional broadcasts.
     * *Lore Clue:* Channel 4 News shows aerial footage of the **Arklay River bridges** and **North Raccoon highway** blocked by military APCs and burning roadblocks. The anchor cuts to static mid-sentence.
  4. **"Food Court / Janitorial Corridors" (Rear Access):**
     * Leads to basement breaker panels, chiller rooms, and the rear drainage conduit connecting to the **Circular River / City Sewer System**.

---

### SCENE 4: STEEL SHUTTERS & EMERGENCY BROADCAST
* **The Announcement (20:15 EST):**
  * All background mall muzak abruptly cuts out. A high-pitched, piercing Emergency Broadcast System tone shrieks across every speaker and radio in the facility.
  * A flat, robotic emergency broadcast replaces the music:
  > *"This is an official emergency alert from the State Governor's Office and federal civil defense authorities. A Level 4 Public Safety Quarantine is now in effect for the entire Raccoon City metropolitan area. All citizens are strongly advised to remain inside their homes, safe shelters, or designated municipal collection zones. Do not travel on public roads. Hostile anarchists, rioters, and individuals exhibiting rabid behavior are roaming the streets. Lock all exterior doors and windows. The city will remain under military containment until dawn."*
* **The Lockdown:**
  * Motorized iron security shutters slam down across the main glass entrances on **Central Street**, skylights, and the parking ramps with deafening hydraulic thuds.
  * Emergency backup lights hum on, casting long amber and red shadows down the corridors.
  * Panic erupts among the remaining ~60 shoppers in the Central Atrium.
* **The Survivors Meet:** The players naturally converge together (drawn by shared competence, calm composure, or proximity during the panic) in the Central Atrium.

---

### SCENE 5: THE FORK IN THE ROAD (The Major Decision)
* **The Security Chief's Announcement:**
  * **Head of Security, Raymond Miller**, climbs onto the edge of the central fountain with a battery-powered megaphone:
  > *"Listen up, everyone! Quiet down! Dispatch just came through on the emergency radio. The city has sent three municipal transit buses to the North Service Alley behind the mall. They are escorted by police cruisers to evacuate citizens south toward the R.P.D. precinct and municipal collection shelters. Anyone who wants out needs to board now! For everyone else—the mall is sealed. We've got food, water, and steel shutters. We can hold the building until morning."*

## 4. CANONICAL MAP-ALIGNED BRANCHES

### BRANCH A: THE NORTH ALLEY EVACUATION BUS (Street-to-Street Survival)
* **The Route:**
  * Party boards **Transit Bus #14**, heading South down **Central Street**, intending to turn West onto **Ennerdale Street** toward the **R.P.D. Precinct (Grid E-13)**.
* **The Complication (The Gridlock Collapse):**
  * Just past **City Hall** near the intersection of **Central & Ennerdale**, the convoy slams into a burning multi-car pileup blocked by an overturned gasoline tanker.
  * Squad car escorts are overwhelmed by a surging horde emerging from **Central Park (Grid E-11)**; the bus driver is pulled through the side window.
* **Immediate Escape Routes:**
  * *Option 1 (South):* Cut through the alleyways behind **Ander's Bar / Jack's Bar** on Ennerdale toward the rear gated entrance of the **R.P.D. Precinct (Grid E-13)**.
  * *Option 2 (West):* Retreat toward the **Raccoon City Hospital (Grid E-8)** or **Kendo Gun Shop (Grid D-13)** perimeter to regroup.

### BRANCH B: FORTIFYING THE GALLERIA (Mall Survival & Sub-Level Escape)
* **The Route:**
  * Players decide the streets are a slaughterhouse and resolve to fortify the Mall with security.
  * Establish a **Safe Room** in the 3rd Floor Security Operations Suite (Item Box & Typewriter location).
* **The Complication (The Inside Collapse):**
  * The main substation grid from **R.C. Power Substation (Grid F-13)** fails, plunging the mall into pitch darkness.
  * Several shoppers who were secretly scratched or bleeding during the initial parking garage panic succumb to the T-Virus and revive as **Shamblers / Crimson Heads** inside the sealed mall.
* **Immediate Escape Routes:**
  * *Option 1 (Sub-Basement):* Descend into the Mall HVAC & Maintenance tunnels to restore the backup diesel generator.
  * *Option 2 (Underground Conduit):* Crack the heavy drainage sluice gate in the sub-basement to escape through the subterranean runoff conduit into the **Circular River / Sewer Network (Grid G-12)**.

## 5. KEY NPCS IN CHAPTER 1
1. **Raymond Miller (Mall Security Chief):** Stubborn, by-the-book ex-cop. Carrying a standard 9mm Handgun and the Mall Master Keycard.
2. **Officer Kevin Vance (Wounded Rookie):** Shaken, moral compass. Needs medical observation. If treated kindly, provides the party with security codes and radio batteries.
3. **Arthur Pendelton (Civilian Pharmacist):** Knowledgeable about chemical synthesis and medical supplies. Can assist the party in identifying medicinal herbs if brought back to him.
4. **Chloe Diaz (Grizzly Outdoors Clerk):** Practical, cynical. Knows the building's maintenance shortcuts and has access to sports gear.

## 6. GM ADJUDICATION & THREAT CARD
* **Active Noise Clock:** Starts at **1/6 (Quiet)**.
  * Kicking down store doors or loud panic: **+1 Tick**.
  * Unsuppressed gunshots during early scuffles: **+1 / +2 Ticks**.
* **Loot Roll Table (Focus TN 10):**
  * *Clean Success:* 1 Combat Knife, 1 Green Herb, or 1 Handgun Box (15 rds 9mm).
  * *Success with Cost:* Yields the item, but makes a loud noise (**+1 Noise Tick**) or breaks a tool.
  * *Failure:* Nothing found; draws the attention of an agitated NPC or security guard.
