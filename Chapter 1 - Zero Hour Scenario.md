# BIOHAZARD: ZERO HOUR (CHAPTER 1 SCENARIO BLUEPRINT)
*A VBG-Z Campaign Opening Module set during the collapse of Raccoon City.*

---

## 1. SCENARIO OVERVIEW & CHRONOLOGY
* **Setting:** The Raccoon Prime Galleria (Multi-tier suburban shopping mall, Northeast Uptown District).
* **Timeline:** September 24, 1998 — 19:45 EST (Hour Zero).
* **Starting Threat Level:** Ambient Noise Clock at **1/6 (Quiet)**. No open combat immediately; social tension, environmental panic, and logistical confusion.
* **Chapter Goal:** Establish survivor connections, scavenge preliminary tools/supplies, observe the escalating quarantine, and make the critical first branch decision: **Board the Evacuation Bus** vs. **Fortify the Mall till Dawn**.

## 3. DETAILED SCENE BREAKDOWN

### SCENE 1: THE CRACK IN THE GLASS (Atmosphere & Introduction)
* **Location:** Central Atrium, Ground Floor (Fountain & Directory Kiosks).
* **Atmosphere:** Fluorescent lights hum with slight electrical flickering. Stores are prematurely pulling down security grates. Shoppers carry heavy grocery bags; murmurs about the canceled High School football game and police cordons on the Arklay perimeter.
* **Intercom Announcement 1 (19:50 EST):**
  > *"Attention valued shoppers. By order of the Raccoon City Police Department and Mall Security, please remain vigilant of your surroundings. If you observe individuals exhibiting extreme agitation, disorientation, or flu-like symptoms, please do not engage. Report immediately to the nearest security kiosk on Level 1."*
* **Player Hook:** Each player establishes why they are here (shopping for emergency canned food, working an evening shift, meeting a contact, waiting out a stalled car, or treating an early medical call).

---

### SCENE 2: THE EAST PARKING LOT STANDOFF (The First Sighting)
* **Location:** East Wing Exit (Glass doors leading to Multi-Story Parking Structure B).
* **The Situation:**
  * Two senior Mall Security guards in yellow-and-black jackets are struggling to chain the heavy glass crash doors shut.
  * Outside in the rain, a dozen angry, pale-faced citizens are shoving against the glass, screaming obscenities and pounding with bare fists. Their movements are jerky and uncoordinated.
  * A line of 30+ frustrated shoppers is blocked from reaching their parked cars, yelling at the guards.
* **The Rookie's Wound (NPC Encounter):**
  * Slumped on a nearby bench is a rookie mall cop, **Officer Kevin Vance** (21 years old), clutching a blood-soaked towel to his right shoulder.
  * A middle-aged civilian pharmacist, **Arthur Pendelton**, is nervously trying to bandage it with duct tape and gauze from a basic wall kit.
  * *Investigation / Medicine Note:* If a player inspects the wound (Focus TN 10), it is clearly a **jagged metal laceration** from a shattered parking gate turnstile, *not* a bite or claw mark. Vance is pale and in shock, mumbling: *"They weren't stopping... one guy just kept pushing his face against the turnstile spike... he didn't even yell..."*

---

### SCENE 3: EXPLORATION & THE 20-MINUTE SCAVENGE WINDOW
* **Parameters:** The party has a brief window before total lockdown. Each player can visit **one** wing or store to make an initial investigation or Focus check.
* **Exploration Locations & Scavenge Opportunities:**
  1. **"Grizzly Outdoors" (Sports & Camping Store - 2nd Floor):**
     * Metal grates half-closed.
     * *Focus TN 10 Scavenge:* Yields a **Hunting Knife (1 Slot, ⬜⬜⬜ Durability)** or **Heavy Crowbar (2 Slots, ⬜⬜⬜ Durability, +1 Breaching)**.
  2. **"Pharm-Plus Express" (1st Floor East):**
     * Ransacked shelves, medicine cabinets locked behind glass.
     * *Finesse / Focus TN 12:* Yields **1 Green Herb (1/4 Slot)** or **1 Basic Bandage Pack (Stops Bleeding)**.
  3. **"RadioShack / Tech Nexus" (Electronics - 1st Floor West):**
     * Bank of demo TVs tuned to regional broadcasts.
     * *Lore Clue:* Channel 4 News shows aerial footage of the Arklay Bridge blocked by heavy military barricades and burning APCs. The news anchor suddenly cuts to static mid-sentence.
  4. **"Food Court Storage / Janitorial Hallway" (Rear Access):**
     * Locked security door. Leads to rear service alleys and basement breaker panels.

---

### SCENE 4: STEEL SHUTTERS & EMERGENCY BROADCAST
* **The Announcement (20:15 EST):**
  * All background mall muzak abruptly cuts out. A high-pitched, piercing Emergency Broadcast System tone shrieks across every speaker and radio in the facility.
  * A flat, robotic emergency broadcast replaces the music:
  > *"This is an official emergency alert from the State Governor's Office and federal civil defense authorities. A Level 4 Public Safety Quarantine is now in effect for the entire Raccoon City metropolitan area. All citizens are strongly advised to remain inside their homes, safe shelters, or designated municipal collection zones. Do not travel on public roads. Hostile anarchists, rioters, and individuals exhibiting rabid behavior are roaming the streets. Lock all exterior doors and windows. The city will remain under military containment until dawn."*
* **The Lockdown:**
  * Motorized iron security shutters slam down across the main glass entrances, skylights, and loading docks with deafening hydraulic thuds.
  * Emergency backup lights hum on, casting long amber and red shadows down the corridors.
  * Panic erupts among the remaining ~60 shoppers in the Central Atrium.
* **The Survivors Meet:** The players naturally gravitate together (drawn by shared competence, calm composure, or proximity during the panic) in the Central Atrium.

---

### SCENE 5: THE FORK IN THE ROAD (The Major Decision)
* **The Security Chief's Announcement:**
  * **Head of Security, Raymond Miller**, climbs onto the edge of the central fountain with a battery-powered megaphone:
  > *"Listen up, everyone! Quiet down! RPD just came through on the trunk line. The city has dispatched three municipal transit buses to the North Service Bay behind the mall. They are escorted by police cruisers to evacuate citizens to the uptown shelters. Anyone who wants out needs to board now! For everyone else—the mall is sealed. We've got food, water, and steel shutters. We can hold the building until morning."*

## 4. THE TWO CAMPAIGN BRANCHES

### BRANCH A: THE NORTH BAY EVACUATION BUS
* **The Premise:** Players choose to get moving rather than stay trapped in a glass-and-steel box.
* **Encounter Setup:**
  * The party boards **Bus #14**. Overcrowded, weeping passengers, rain lashing against the windows.
  * Two squad cars escort the bus out onto Central Avenue.
* **The Complication (The Inevitable Gridlock):**
  * 10 minutes into the ride, the convoy hits a catastrophic multi-car pileup at the intersection of Ennerdale and Mission Street.
  * Gunfire breaks out ahead; infected swarm the escort cars; the bus driver is bitten or crashes into a light pole.
  * *Next Stage:* Street-to-street survival, navigating through alleyways toward the RPD Precinct or General Hospital.

### BRANCH B: FORTIFYING THE GALLERIA (MALL SURVIVAL)
* **The Premise:** Players decide the streets are suicide and resolve to fortify the Galleria with security.
* **Encounter Setup:**
  * Securing the service corridors, locking off the loading bay, and establishing a **Safe Room** in the 3rd Floor Security Management Suite (Item Box & Typewriter location).
* **The Complication (The Inside Breach):**
  * The mall is not as secure as they think: power generators in the basement fail, plunging the building into pitch darkness.
  * Worse: shoppers bitten outside during the early scuffles begin dying of their infections—and reviving as **Crimson Heads / Shamblers** inside the locked building.
  * *Next Stage:* Dungeon-crawling through the multi-level mall, restoring the backup generator, and clearing the basement maintenance tunnels to escape into the city sewers.

## 5. KEY NPCS IN CHAPTER 1
1. **Raymond Miller (Mall Security Chief):** Stubborn, by-the-book ex-cop. Wants to maintain order and protect the mall property. Carries a standard 9mm Handgun and the Master Keycard.
2. **Officer Kevin Vance (Wounded Rookie):** Shaken, moral compass. Needs medical observation. If treated kindly, provides the party with security codes and radio batteries.
3. **Arthur Pendelton (Civilian Pharmacist):** Knowledgeable about chemical synthesis and medical supplies. Can assist the party in identifying medicinal herbs if brought back to him.
4. **Chloe Diaz (Grizzly Outdoors Clerk):** Practical, cynical. Knows the building's maintenance shortcuts and has access to sports gear.

## 6. GM ADJUDICATION & THREAT CARD
* **Active Noise Clock:** Starts at **1/6 (Quiet)**.
  * Kicking down store doors or loud panic: **+1 Tick**.
  * Unsuppressed gunshots during early scuffles: **+1 / +2 Ticks**.
* **Loot Roll Table (Focus TN 10):**
  * *Clean Success:* 1 Combat Knife, 1 Green Herb, or 1 Handgun Box (15 rds 9mm).
  * *Success with Cost:* Yields the item, but makes a loud noise (**+1 Noise Tick**) or breaks a tool.
  * *Failure:* Nothing found; draws the attention of an agitated NPC or security guard.
