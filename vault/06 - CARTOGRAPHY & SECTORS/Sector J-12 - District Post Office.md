## 📢 GM Read-Aloud Box
> *Sector J-12 is dominated by the monolithic brick edifice of the Raccoon District Post Office and municipal sorting facility. Fleets of white-and-blue mail vans sit bumper-to-bumper in the flooded loading bays, their cargo doors hanging open with rain-soaked parcels spilling into the street. Blue emergency beacons flash from a disabled police squad car wedged into the sorting annex gates.*

---

## 🧭 Tactical Landmarks & Search Points
* **Main Sorting Concourse:** High sorting racks and motorized conveyor belts (Grants +2 stealth against roamers).
* **Fleet Service Bay:** Contains automotive tools, tire irons, battery jump-leads, and diesel canisters.
* **Postal Safe:** Lockbox containing registered mail caches (9mm ammunition boxes and cash vouchers).
