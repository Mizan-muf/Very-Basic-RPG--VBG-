## 📢 GM Read-Aloud Box
> *Set against the foothills of the Arklay Mountains, Sector J-10 houses the prestigious Raccoon City Antiquities Museum. Gothic limestone arches, wrought-iron gargoyles, and pre-war clocktower architecture overlook manicured courtyards. Subterranean 19th-century municipal aqueducts run deep beneath the foundations, directly linking the museum's basement vaults to the historic theater district.*

---

## 🧭 Tactical Landmarks & Features
* **Antiquities Courtyard:** Ingress point from the Subterranean Ruins ([[Zone 3.4 - Subterranean Ancient Ruins Descent]]).
* **Clocktower Annex:** High-ground observation tower with panoramic sightlines across northern Raccoon City.
* **Pre-War Relic Vaults:** Displays containing medieval armor, antique firearms, and historical city blueprints.
