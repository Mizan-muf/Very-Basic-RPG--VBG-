## 🎬 ACT I: HOUR ZERO — THE GALLERIA LOCKDOWN
* **Date:** Friday, September 25, 1998 (19:45 – 20:45 EST)
* **Location:** [[Sector J-13 - Raccoon Prime Galleria]]

### Scene 1: The Ambient Cracks (Friday, September 25, 1998 — 19:45 EST)
The campaign opens inside the **Raccoon Prime Galleria** ([[Sector J-13 - Raccoon Prime Galleria]]), on the east bank of the Circular River. It is a rainy Friday evening in late September. Emergency sirens wail continuously along the distant Arklay mountain perimeter, and local news broadcasts on display televisions intermittently flicker with static reports of quarantined roadblocks.

The mall is still open, but the atmosphere is tense. Shoppers are scattered across the ground floor promenade, unaware that patient zero has already entered the city transit grid.

* **Primary Narrative Focus:** The party members meet or establish their starting positions within the mall during normal business hours.
* **Explorable Zone References:**
  * Survivors on the ground floor start in [[Zone 1.1 - 1st Floor Central Atrium & Retail]].
  * Survivors browsing upper specialty shops start in [[Zone 1.2 - 2nd Floor Mezzanine & Outfitters]].
* **Intercom Broadcast #1:**
  > *"Attention valued shoppers. Security requests that all patrons remain vigilant. Please report any disoriented, highly agitated, or flu-symptomatic individuals to mall security dispatch immediately."*

---

### Scene 2: The Parking Gate Standoff & The Rookie’s Wound (Friday, September 25, 1998 — 20:00 EST)
A sudden security breach occurs at the lower freight delivery dock. Screams echo up the escalator wells.

* **Primary Narrative Focus:**
  * [[Officer Kevin Vance]] has sustained a severe laceration across his shoulder while attempting to secure Parking Gate B.
  * [[Pharmacist Arthur Pendelton]] is attempting field triage near the Central Atrium fountain.
* **Explorable Zone References:**
  * The crisis centers on [[Zone 1.4 - Basement & East Loading Bay]].
  * Medical supplies are located in [[Zone 1.1 - 1st Floor Central Atrium & Retail]] (Pharm-Plus).
* **Intercom Broadcast #2:**
  > *"All security personnel report to Sub-Level 2 freight gates. Code Orange. All civilian patrons please move in an orderly fashion toward designated emergency exits."*

---

### Scene 3: The 20-Minute Scavenge Window (Friday, September 25, 1998 — 20:05 – 20:25 EST)
The mall perimeter is locked into a 20-minute countdown before central civic automation seals all exterior hurricane shutters.

* **Primary Narrative Focus:**
  * The party has exactly 20 minutes (2 in-game exploratory rounds) to secure weapons, tools, medical supplies, and keys before total lockdown.
  * Every loud action or firearm discharge increments the **Area Noise Clock**.
* **Explorable Zone References:**
  * Heavy tools and firearms can be scavenged in [[Zone 1.2 - 2nd Floor Mezzanine & Outfitters]].
  * Security monitors and keycards are in [[Zone 1.3 - 3rd Floor Security Operations & Safe Room]].

---

### Scene 4: Level 4 Quarantine & The Steel Lockdown (Friday, September 25, 1998 — 20:25 EST)
The automated klaxons sound across the Galleria. Heavy steel fire shutters slam downward over every public glass entrance and skylight.

* **Primary Narrative Focus:**
  * The mall is plunged into emergency red auxiliary lighting.
  * Exterior escape via front doors is severed; only staff freight bays and service vectors remain.
* **Intercom Broadcast #3 (Final Automated Transmission):**
  > *"Federal Directive 104-B is now active. Raccoon City metropolitan district is under mandatory Class 4 Biohazard quarantine. All civic transit is suspended. Remain inside your structure and await military containment units."*

---
