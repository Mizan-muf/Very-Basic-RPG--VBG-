## 🎬 ACT I: HOUR ZERO — THE GALLERIA LOCKDOWN
* **Date:** Friday, September 25, 1998 (19:45 – 20:35 EST)
* **Location:** [[Sector J-13 - Raccoon Prime Galleria]]

### Scene 1: The Ambient Cracks (Friday, September 25, 1998 — 19:45 EST)
The campaign opens inside the **Raccoon Prime Galleria** ([[Sector J-13 - Raccoon Prime Galleria]]), on the east bank of the Circular River. It is a rainy Friday evening in late September. Emergency sirens wail continuously along the distant Arklay mountain perimeter, and local news broadcasts on display televisions intermittently flicker with static reports of quarantined roadblocks.

The mall is still open, but the atmosphere is tense. Shoppers are scattered across the ground floor promenade, unaware that patient zero has already entered the city transit grid.

* **Primary Narrative Focus:** The party members meet or establish their starting positions within the mall during normal business hours.
* **Explorable Zone References:**
  * Survivors on the ground floor start in [[Zone 1.1 - 1st Floor Central Atrium & Retail]].
  * Survivors browsing upper specialty shops start in [[Zone 1.2 - 2nd Floor Mezzanine & Outfitters]].
* **Intercom Broadcast #1:**
  > *"Attention valued shoppers. Security requests that all patrons remain vigilant. Please report any disoriented, highly agitated, or flu-symptomatic individuals to mall security dispatch immediately."*

---

### Scene 2: The Parking Gate Standoff & The Rookie’s Wound (Friday, September 25, 1998 — 20:00 EST)
A loud disturbance breaks out at the basement parking concourse. Security personnel begin manually closing the heavy steel roll-up gates leading to Parking Structure B against a mob of agitated citizens banging on the exterior glass.

* **Primary Narrative Focus:**
  * Rookie security officer [[Officer Kevin Vance]] sustains a severe, bleeding laceration across his shoulder.
  * [[Pharmacist Arthur Pendelton]] rushes over with medical supplies from Pharm-Plus.
  * The players have an opportunity to assist with first aid (`Focus TN 10`), establishing early cooperation and confirming the wound is jagged torn metal from a broken turnstile—not a human bite.
* **Zone Location:** [[Zone 1.4 - Basement & East Loading Bay]].

---

### Scene 3: The 20-Minute Scavenge Window (Friday, September 25, 1998 — 20:05 – 20:25 EST)
Following the gate incident, security chief Raymond Miller urges all remaining patrons to prepare to leave or shelter while transit authorities assess street safety. Survivors have a narrow 20-minute window of liberty before full containment measures initiate.

* **Primary Narrative Focus:** Survivors scrounge for basic survival gear, tools, and medical herbs before the shops are shuttered.
* **Accessible Exploration Zones (Choose 1-2 to investigate):**
  * [[Zone 1.1 - 1st Floor Central Atrium & Retail]] — First aid herbs at *Pharm-Plus*, bladed tools at *King’s Cutlery*, commercial gas cutoffs at the *Food Court*.
  * [[Zone 1.2 - 2nd Floor Mezzanine & Outfitters]] — Hunting bows, crowbars, and tactical gear at *Grizzly Outdoors*; emergency communications at *Tech Nexus*; dark screening rooms at *Raccoon Cineplex*.
  * [[Zone 1.3 - 3rd Floor Security Operations & Safe Room]] — CCTV camera feeds, manual typewriter, and the locked security arms locker.

---

### Scene 4: Level 4 Quarantine & The Steel Lockdown (Friday, September 25, 1998 — 20:25 EST)
At 20:25 EST, all retail music abruptly cuts out. The citywide emergency broadcast alarm blares across the mall public address system:

> *"Civil Emergency Alert. By order of the State Governor and Raccoon City Municipal Council, a Level 4 Sanitary Quarantine is now in effect. All citizens are instructed to immediately seek shelter in their homes or designated municipal staging facilities. Avoid all aggressive individuals. All perimeter gates will be sealed until dawn."*

Hydraulic sirens sound as heavy steel riot shutters drop over every exterior glass entrance, skylight, and parking gate. The mall is sealed from the outside world. Survivors are herded by security back to the central atrium.

* **Zone Transition:** Convergence back to [[Zone 1.1 - 1st Floor Central Atrium & Retail]].

---

### Scene 5: The Major Fork in the Road (Friday, September 25, 1998 — 20:30 EST)
Security Chief Miller gathers the survivors at the atrium and announces that municipal transit authorities have staged three emergency evacuation buses at the North Loading Bay under police escort to transport civilians west across the bridge toward the safety of the downtown precinct perimeter.

Survivors must make their first critical campaign decision:

* **Branch A (The Evacuation Bus):**
  * Survivors board Transit Bus #14 idling in [[Zone 1.4 - Basement & East Loading Bay]].
  * Narrative continues in **[[Node 2.1 - Branch A (The Bus Convoy Route)]]** *(20:45 EST)*.
* **Branch B (Holding the Galleria):**
  * Survivors refuse the bus, choosing to fortify the 3rd-floor security suite ([[Zone 1.3 - 3rd Floor Security Operations & Safe Room]]).
  * Narrative continues in **[[Node 2.2 - Branch B (The East River Run)]]** *(20:50 EST)*.
