## 📢 GM Read-Aloud Box
> *A deep, terrifying vibration rumbles up through the steel rails and into your boots. From the western mountain curve, twin blinding high-beam headlamps tear through the downpour. An unmanned diesel freight locomotive, train horn locked in a deafening continuous blast, roars down Track 3 at 60 MPH. It violently impacts the lead Licker as the beast leaps across the rails, pulverizing the monster against a concrete stanchion in a shower of sparks, gore, and twisted iron!*

## 🎲 Dynamic Environmental Impact
* **The Noise Surge:**
  * The deafening collision and locked horn **increase the Area Noise Clock by +2 ticks** (stacks with prior noise).
  * Any remaining roamer hordes in the rail yards are drawn toward the burning train derailment.
* **The Breached Perimeter:**
  * A derailed boxcar smashes through the heavy industrial chain-link security fence, warping open the freight doors of **[[Zone 3.3 - Central Cinema Rear Freight Bay (South Entry)]]**.
  * Survivors have an immediate covered ramp to scramble into the cinema interior!
