## 📢 GM Read-Aloud Box
> *You step out into the rain-swept switching yard. Four parallel sets of steel tracks run beneath massive green-painted steel signal bridges and maintenance trusses thirty feet overhead. Floodlights flicker erratically against grease-blackened ballast stones. High above on the steel framework, long red, glistening appendages slither silently across the girders—two blind [[Licker (Evolved Predator)|Lickers]], heads twitching as they listen to every rain drop striking the iron rails.*

---

## 🤫 Acoustic Stealth & Licker Sensory Matrix
* **Hyper-Acoustic Echolocation:**
  * Lickers are completely blind but instantly pinpoint sounds in the **Far (20–60ft)** distance band.
  * Moving through the yard without alerting them requires a **Finesse TN 13 (Stealth)** check.
  * *Unsuppressed Gunfire / Shouting:* Automatically triggers a **Lethal Ceiling Pounce** (Forces **Finesse TN 16** or take 2 Strain + Bleeding).
* **Decoy Tactics:**
  * Firing a flare gun or throwing a metallic wrench into an empty freight car diverts both Lickers for 2 turns.

## 🧩 Track Switching Lever Puzzle
* **Objective:** Align Track Switch #3 to unlock the siding gate leading to Central Cinema.
* **Mechanics:** Requires **Force TN 13** to throw the rusted iron lever or lubricating the pivot with aerosol oil (**Focus TN 10**).
