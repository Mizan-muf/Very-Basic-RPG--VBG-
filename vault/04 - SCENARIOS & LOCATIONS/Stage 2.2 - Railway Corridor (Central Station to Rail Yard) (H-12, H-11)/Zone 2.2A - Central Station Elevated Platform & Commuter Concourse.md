## 🧭 Environment & Commuter Inundation
An elevated concrete platform flanked by twin tracks and cast-iron Victorian arches. Heavy rain slants under the canopy. Stalled commuter express #412 sits idling with headlights cutting through steam.

* **Infected Presence:** 8 [[Commuter Shambler]]s wander the turnstiles and baggage claims.
* **Tactical Choice:**
  * **Option 1 (Stealth Bypass):** Navigate through dark baggage cars (Requires **Finesse TN 12** to step over luggage without rattling metal).
  * **Option 2 (Platform Sweep):** Engage with small arms / melee. Every shot fired advances Noise Clock by +1 and alerts roosting predators in the yards ahead.
