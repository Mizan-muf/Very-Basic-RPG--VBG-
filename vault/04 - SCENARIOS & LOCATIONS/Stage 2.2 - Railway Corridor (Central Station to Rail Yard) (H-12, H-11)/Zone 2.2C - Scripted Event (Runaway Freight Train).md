## 💥 SCRIPTED MOMENT: THE IRON JUGGERNAUT
As the survivors engage or evade the predators in the switching yard, a blaring horn shrieks from the southern tunnel. Freight Locomotive #809—its crew cabin splattered with blood—comes careening down Track 2 at 60 MPH.

* **Licker Pulverization:** The locomotive violently plows into the lead [[Licker (Evolved Predator)]] as it prepares to pounce, smashing it against the concrete stanchion in a shower of gore (Instant Kill).
* **Noise Clock Surge:**
  * The deafening roar, horn screech, and metallic collision **increases Noise Clock by +2**.
  * Any prior noise made by the party stacks with this increase.
* **Debris & Evasion:** Survivors on or near Track 2 must roll **Finesse TN 14** to dive clear of flying ballast and severed rail ties.
* **The Breached Gate:** The derailed rear hopper car smashes through the perimeter chain-link fence, opening a direct ramp into [[Zone 3.3 - Central Cinema Rear Freight Bay (South Entry)]] (`Sector H-11`).
