## 👅 APEX PREDATOR THREAT: THE LICKER TRUSSES
Two [[Licker (Evolved Predator)]]s cling to the overhead signal bridge 25 feet above Track 2 and Track 4. Their elongated brains glisten in the rain; flayed muscular skin twitches at every droplet.

* **Hyper-Acoustic Stealth Rules:**
  * Walking quietly across wet ballast requires **Finesse TN 13**.
  * Firing a firearm automatically fails stealth, causing an immediate vertical ambush from Close band.
  * Dropping a tool or tripping on rails advances Noise Clock by +1.

## 🧩 Track Switch Puzzle
To open the manual derailer gate leading into Central Cinema's siding, survivors must align Track Switch Lever #3:
* **Lever 1 (Mainline North):** Locked by rusted bolt (Requires **Force TN 14** or Railway Crowbar).
* **Lever 3 (Cinema Freight Siding):** Shifts rails into alignment with Cinema Bay door.
