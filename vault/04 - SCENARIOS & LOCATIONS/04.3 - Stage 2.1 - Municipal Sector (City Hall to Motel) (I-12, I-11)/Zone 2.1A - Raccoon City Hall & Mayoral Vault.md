## 🧭 Environment & Layout
Neoclassical marble halls, vaulted ceilings, and walnut council desks. Emergency red emergency lights bathe the foyer. Zombie density is low (3 wandering city clerks), but all northern exits are sealed by high-voltage magnetic security shutters.

## 🧩 Survival Horror Puzzles

### 1. The Electrical Breaker & Generator Relay
* **Location:** Sub-Basement Maintenance Tunnel.
* **Objective:** Restore auxiliary power to Mayor Warren's private elevator.
* **Mechanic:** Survivors must balance a three-phase breaker switch matrix to output exactly 240 Volts without blowing the 15A main fuse:
  * Switch 1 (Civic Grid): +120V (Requires **Focus TN 12**)
  * Switch 2 (Annex Battery): +70V
  * Switch 3 (Emergency Dynamo): +50V
  * *Failure:* Sparks shower the room, advancing Noise Clock by +1 and dealing 1 Strain to the operator.

### 2. The Mayoral Bronze Clockwork Relief
* **Location:** 2nd Floor Mayoral Antechamber.
* **Objective:** Unlock the hidden wall vault behind the bronze mural of Raccoon City's founding fathers.
* **Riddle:** Inscribed on the mayoral mahogany desk:  
  > *"When the clock strikes dusk and the stag faces the Arklay peaks, the city gates shall yield."*
* **Solution:** Turn the clock hands to **08:15** and rotate the brass stag relief **90 degrees West**.
* **Reward:** Unlocks Mayor Warren's secret northern garden exit and reveals:
  * **Mayoral Keycard (Level C)**
  * **Shotgun Ammo Box** (⬜⬜⬜⬜⬜⬜ / 6 Shells)
  * Collectible Memo: *Mayor Warren's Emergency Directive*
