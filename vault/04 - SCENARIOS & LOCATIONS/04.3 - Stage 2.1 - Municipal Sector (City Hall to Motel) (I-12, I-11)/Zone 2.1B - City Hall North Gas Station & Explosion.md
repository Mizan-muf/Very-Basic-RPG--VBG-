## 📢 GM Read-Aloud Box
> *Exiting the mayoral gardens brings you into the rainy asphalt lot of the Stag Petroleum station. A crashed police cruiser rests crumpled against Pump #3, its engine hissing white smoke across pools of spilled gasoline. Just as you step onto the forecourt, an electrical arc flashes from the snapped canopy wire—igniting the vapor in a deafening, blinding blast that tears the station canopy from its steel pillars. Flames roar thirty feet into the stormy sky, illuminating the staggering silhouettes of burning infected lunging from the shadows.*

---

## 💥 SCRIPTED EVENT: THE TANKER IGNITION
As survivors push through the gas station forecourt toward the Motel, an abandoned police cruiser slides on fuel, crashing into Pump #3. Sparks ignite a ruptured 10,000-gallon underground reservoir.

* **The Blast:** A blinding fireball engulfs the forecourt.
* **Immediate Mechanics:**
  * All survivors within 40ft must make a **Finesse TN 14** check to dive behind concrete barriers (Failure: 1 Armor / 1 Strain from concussive force).
  * **NOISE CLOCK AUTOMATICALLY SPIKES TO 6/6 (MAX ALERT).**

---

## 🧟 THE ENCOUNTER: HORDE INUNDATION
The explosion illuminates the entire sector and reverberates across downtown. Within 60 seconds:
* 6 [[Burning Roamer]]s stagger through the flames toward the party.
* 1 [[Urban Shambler]] Swarm closes off the rear City Hall gate.
* **Escape Requirement:** Survivors must breach the wire-mesh gate into [[Zone 2.1C - St. Michael Motel & Room 104 Safe Room]] (Requires **Force TN 14** or bolt cutters / shotgun blast to padlock) before being enveloped.
