## 📢 GM Read-Aloud Box
> *The yellow municipal transit bus tears out of the loading bay into the torrential rain, wipers slapping frantically against the windshield. Outside, the streets of Raccoon City are a nightmare of stalled sedans, flashing hazard lights, and running silhouettes. Inside the packed, swaying passenger cabin, the atmosphere is suffocating with fear. Suddenly, from the shadowed rear seats, a chilling gurgle breaks out—a bitten civilian collapses, convulses, and rises with milky, predatory eyes.*

---

## 🎲 Dynamic Vehicle Combat & Panic Checks
* **Tight Quarters:** The aisle is narrow (Melee band only). Ranged attacks without Point-Blank traits suffer -2 penalty.
* **In-Cabin Outbreak:**
  * 1 freshly turned [[Urban Shambler]] lunges from the rear row.
  * **Lunging Grab:** Forces a **Finesse TN 13** or **Force TN 14** check to avoid being pinned against the window.
* **Driver Panic Check:**
  * The driver screams, losing control of the steering wheel.
  * A survivor must rush to the driver's cab and make a **Force or Finesse TN 14** check to stabilize the bus.
  * *Failure:* The bus crashes through an intersection roadblock at 45 MPH, triggering [[Node 2.3 - The Intersection Crash Site]].
