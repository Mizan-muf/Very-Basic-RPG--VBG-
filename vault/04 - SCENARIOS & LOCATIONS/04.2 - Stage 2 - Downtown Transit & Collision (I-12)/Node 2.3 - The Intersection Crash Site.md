## 📢 GM Read-Aloud Box
> *The intersection of Mission and Lombard is an inferno. Bus #14 lies overturned on its side, black smoke billowing into the night sky as burning gasoline spreads across the wet asphalt. Car alarms shriek in deafening discord, and the flashing headlights of abandoned police cruisers cut through the rising fog. From every side street, dozens of shambling figures are drawn toward the blaze like moths to a flame. You have less than two minutes before the crossroads is completely enveloped.*

## 🔍 Wreckage Search (High Risk)
Searching the burning bus interior while the clock ticks:
* Requires a **Finesse TN 12** check to enter the smoke-filled chassis.
* **Loot:** **Riot Shotgun Shells** (4 Shells), **Emergency Flare** (1 Magnesium flare), **First Aid Spray** (1 Canister).
