## 📢 GM Read-Aloud Box
> *The alley opens onto the rain-slick stone embankment of the Circular River. Behind you, the East Substation explodes in a shower of brilliant green electrical sparks, cutting power to the eastern streetlights and plunging the riverside into near-total blackness. The river churns below with dark mountain runoff. Across the water, the neon skyline of downtown flickers out district by district. From the shadows of the storm drainage culverts, low, guttural growls echo.*

---

## 🎲 Riverfront Hazards & Encounters
* **Zero Visibility / Heavy Rain:** All ranged attack rolls without a flashlight suffer a -2 penalty.
* **Pack Ambush:**
  * 2 infected K-9 Cerberus dogs bound out from beneath the bridge arches.
  * **Sprinting Pounce:** Forces **Finesse TN 15** to evade. Failure: Knocked prone and takes 1 Strain.
* **Sewer Culvert Grating:**
  * Survivors can bypass the street dogs by prying open a storm runoff grating (Requires **Force TN 13** with a crowbar/fire axe).
