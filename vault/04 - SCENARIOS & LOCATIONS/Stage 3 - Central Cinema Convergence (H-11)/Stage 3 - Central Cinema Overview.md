## 🗺️ Reconvergence Architecture
Regardless of whether survivors took **Pathway 1 (City Hall / Gas Station / Motel)** or **Pathway 2 (Central Station / Rail Yard / Runaway Train)**, both vectors reconverge inside the art-deco structure of **Central Cinema (`H-11`)**.

1. **[[Zone 3.1 - Central Cinema Lobby & Marquee (East Entry)]]**: Grand velvet foyer, concessions counter, shattered glass ticket booth.
2. **[[Zone 3.2 - Central Cinema Projection Booth & Auditoriums]]**: Two screening halls (Auditorium A & B) and the upper 35mm projection booth safe haven.
3. **[[Zone 3.3 - Central Cinema Rear Freight Bay (South Entry)]]**: Industrial roll-up door, reel storage, and the emergency fire escape vector heading toward the R.P.D. precinct perimeter (`Sector E-12`).

---

## 📽️ Key Objectives & Puzzles
* **Auditorium A Projector Relay:** Playing the emergency civic evacuation reel on the main screen distracts roamer clusters in the seats.
* **Safe Room in Projection Booth:** Contains 35mm film logs, secure lock, Green & Red Herbs, and typewriter save terminal.
