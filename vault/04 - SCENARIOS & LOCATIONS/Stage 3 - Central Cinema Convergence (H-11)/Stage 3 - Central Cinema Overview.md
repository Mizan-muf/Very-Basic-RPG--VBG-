## 🧭 Narrative Context: Surface Grid Collapse
All surface streets surrounding Sector H-11—Ennerdale Street, Mission Avenue, and the elevated highway bypass—are completely impassable. Tanker fires from [[Zone 2.1B - City Hall North Gas Station & Explosion]] and the roaring derailment at [[Zone 2.2C - Scripted Event (Runaway Freight Train)]] have drawn hundreds of infected into the surrounding intersections.

The historic **Central Cinema (`H-11`)**—an aging, low-occupancy art-deco picture palace built in 1928—stands as the only secure, fortified shelter in the sector. Because it was an off-peak rainy Friday evening showing vintage reels, the interior is nearly empty of civilian infected (only 6–8 total roamers inside the vast building).

## 🧩 The Item-Based Puzzle: The Proscenium Optical Relay
Surface escape is impossible. The only viable path forward is an ancient municipal passage built into the 19th-century foundation beneath the cinema's main stage.

### Required Key Items:
1. **Silver Film Canister ("The Alchemist of Arklay 1934"):** Found in the locked Manager's Office Wall Safe in [[Zone 3.3 - Central Cinema Rear Freight Bay (South Entry)]]. *(Safe combination `07 - 24` is deduced from the lobby movie premiere poster).*
2. **Brass Projection Lens & Prism:** Recovered from the optical workbench in [[Zone 3.2 - Central Cinema Projection Booth & Auditoriums]].

### Puzzle Resolution:
* Survivors install the **Brass Prism** into the 35mm carbon-arc projector and thread the **Silver Film Reel**.
* Engaging the projector casts a high-intensity geometric light beam through the darkened auditorium, striking two photoelectric copper receiver eyes on the stage proscenium arch.
* The hydraulic stage floor drops 15 feet into the foundations, unsealing the heavy limestone archway of [[Zone 3.4 - Subterranean Ancient Ruins Descent]] leading directly to [[Sector J-10 - Raccoon City Museum]].
