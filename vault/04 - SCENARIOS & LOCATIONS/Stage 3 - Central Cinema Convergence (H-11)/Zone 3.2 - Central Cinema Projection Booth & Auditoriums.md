## 📽️ AUDITORIUM A & B (THE SCREENING HALLS)
Raked theater seating bathed in flickering silver light from an idling carbon-arc projector. 
* **Tactical Encounter:** 6 shamblers seated among the theater chairs rise when the aisles are entered.
* **Environmental Action:** Survivors can trigger the curtain hoist (Requires **Force TN 12**) to drop the heavy asbestos stage curtain, pinning 3 shamblers simultaneously.

## 🛡️ THE PROJECTION BOOTH SAFE ROOM
Accessible via the cast-iron spiral staircase behind the snack bar.
* **Heavy Iron Door:** Deadbolts from the inside.
* **Loot & Cache:**
  * **Custom Shotgun Barrel Extension:** Weapon upgrade (+1 on Far range rolls).
  * **Green Herb + Blue Herb Pots.**
  * **Typewriter Terminal & Ribbon.**
