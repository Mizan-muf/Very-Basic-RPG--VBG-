## Tactical Summary
The sprawling steel-and-glass transit concourse of Raccoon Central Station. Stalled diesel passenger locomotives block the northern tracks, while luggage carts, turnstiles, and collapsed ticket booths offer heavy cover.

### Areas & Vector Points
1. **Ticket Concourse (Ground Floor):** High-ceiling echo zone (Noise +1 on all firearms). Multiple infected commuters trapped in turnstile lanes.
2. **Subway Platform & Maintenance Bay:** Underground track leading toward western subway lines. 
3. **Train Shed Service Gate:** Exit point connecting into western downtown corridors toward the R.P.D. precinct.

### Key Resources & Loot
* **Station Master Office:** Keycard (Subway Gate), 1x Shotgun Shells (Box of 4), 1x First Aid Spray.
* **Maintenance Trolley:** Steel Crowbar, Signal Flares (x2).
