## Overview & Tactical Briefing
Central Station serves as the primary railway transit hub connecting Raccoon City to the outer Arklay industrial lines. By 22:00 EST on September 25, 1998, the main concourse is littered with abandoned luggage, derailed freight wagons, and barricaded ticket gates.

### Key Objectives & Hazards
1. **The Rail Platform (22:00 EST):** Navigate past stalled freight trains to find switching keys.
2. **Noise Hazard:** Squealing rail iron and steam vents maintain Noise at 4/6.
3. **Connections:** Access to City Hall West Service Gates and the tracks leading toward Kendo Gun Shop.
