## Tactical Summary
A wide ceremonial plaza paved in decorative stone, dominated by the bronze municipal memorial fountain. Burned-out patrol cruisers and wooden police barricades form a makeshift labyrinth across the square.

### Hazards & Encounters
* **Plaza Chokepoints:** 6–8 [[Urban Shambler]] roamers scattered around the fountain.
* **Sniper Blind / Overlook:** City Hall portico balcony provides high ground for ranged weapons (Handgun / Rifle).
* **Emergency Supplies:** Police cruiser trunk (Force TN 14 or Crowbar: 2x 9mm Handgun Ammo boxes, 1x Combat Knife).
