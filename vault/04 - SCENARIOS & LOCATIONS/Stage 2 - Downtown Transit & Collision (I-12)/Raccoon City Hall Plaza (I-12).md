## 📢 GM Read-Aloud Box
> *Rising above Mission Street stands the grand neoclassical facade of Raccoon City Hall. Broad marble stairs lead up to a columned portico beneath a massive bronze relief of the city seal. A circular memorial fountain in the center of the plaza churns with muddy rainwater and floating autumn leaves. The bronze-reinforced oak entrance doors are deadbolted from within, but auxiliary security floodlights hum on emergency power.*

## 🎲 Plaza Crossing & Breach Mechanics
* **Plaza Crossing:** Dash 60ft across the open plaza dodging roamer clusters (Requires **Finesse TN 12** or expending 1 Shotgun/Area blast to suppress).
* **Breaching the East Portico:**
  * **Option A (Forced Entry):** Requires **Force TN 15** (Fire Axe / Crowbar grants +2).
  * **Option B (Electronic Override):** Requires **Focus TN 14** to hotwire the electronic card reader bypass.
