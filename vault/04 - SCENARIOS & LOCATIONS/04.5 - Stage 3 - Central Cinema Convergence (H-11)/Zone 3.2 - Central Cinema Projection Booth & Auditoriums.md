## 🛡️ THE PROJECTION BOOTH (OFFICIAL SAFE ROOM)
Located at the top of the iron spiral staircase above the balcony.
* **Security:** Solid steel fire door deadbolts from the inside.
* **Safe Room Ambience:** Warm glow of amber vacuum tubes, rhythmic hum of cooling fans. Suppresses Area Noise Clock.
* **Facilities & Storage:**
  * **Typewriter Terminal & Ink Ribbon:** Game save & dossier log.
  * **Botanical Supply:** Green Herb + Blue Herb planters on the fire escape windowsill.
  * **Item Cache:** **Custom Shotgun Barrel Extension** (+1 on Far range attack rolls), Box of 12-Gauge Shotgun Shells (6 Shells).
  * **Workbench Item:** **Brass Projection Lens & Prism** (Required for the Optical Relay Puzzle).

## 🎭 AUDITORIUM 1 & THE PROSCENIUM STAGE
A 400-seat theater flanked by gold-leaf relief pillars.
* **Enemies:** 3 sedentary shamblers slumped in aisle seats; they only stir if illuminated by direct flashlight beams.
* **The Stage Mechanism:** The proscenium arch holds two copper receiver eyes. When illuminated by the aligned projector beam, the central stage drops 15 feet into the sub-basement ruins.

---

## 🧩 The Proscenium Optical Alignment
* **Objective:** Thread the **Silver Film Canister** into the 35mm carbon-arc projector, mount the **Brass Projection Lens & Prism**, and calibrate the twin focal beams.
* **Skill Check:** Requires **Focus TN 12** to adjust the brass prism thumbscrews until the beam hits both stage sensor eyes simultaneously.
* **Result:** The proscenium stage groans and lowers 15 feet into the foundations, revealing [[Zone 3.4 - Subterranean Ancient Ruins Descent]].
