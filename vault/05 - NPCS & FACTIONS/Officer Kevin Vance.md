## 💉 Triage & Escort Mechanics
* **Field Triage Check:**
  * Requires a **Focus TN 12 (Medicine)** check using a **Green Herb (G)** or **First Aid Spray**.
  * *Success:* Halts the Bleeding condition; Vance can walk with support.
  * *Failure:* Vance gains +1 Infection Stage every 30 minutes unless administered a `G+B` herb mixture.
* **Escort Benefit:**
  * While conscious, Vance provides the party with his **Keycard Level B** and knowledge of the Galleria's underground freight layout.
  * In combat, if handed a weapon and calmed (**Focus TN 11**), he can fire covering shots (1d20+1 Handgun check, Close range).
