# 🧪 CHEMICAL SUPPLIES, PHARMACEUTICALS & COMPOUNDING REAGENTS

*VBG-Z Master Specification for Chemical Crafting, Ammunition Synthesis, Bio-Reagents, and Field Pharmaceuticals.*

---

## ⚗️ 1. BASE REAGENTS & SOLVENTS

* **Standard Chemical Fluid (1/4 Slot):** A pressurized bottle of distilled alcohol, mineral oil, and chemical stabilizers. Combined with Green Herbs to create First Aid Spray, or with Gunpowder to produce specialized propellants.
* **High-Grade Chemical Fluid (1/4 Slot):** Industrial-grade concentrated solvent and nitric acid derivative. Used to synthesize High-Grade Explosives, 40mm Grenade rounds, or concentrated Antiviral Suppressants.
* **Denatured Alcohol / Surgical Spirits (1/4 Slot):** 99% isopropyl alcohol used for sterilizing lacerations, cleaning lab glassware, or fueling improvised Molotov cocktails.
* **Surgical Ether / Chloroform Ampoules (1/4 Slot):** Volatile anesthetic fluid used in medical sedation or as a fast-evaporating solvent.
* **Sulfuric & Battery Acid Vials (1/4 Slot):** Highly corrosive electrolyte fluid scavenged from vehicle batteries. Inflicts **Acid Burn** on skin/armor or is compounded into Acid Rounds.
* **Liquid Nitrogen Canister (1 Slot):** Pressurized cryogenic refrigerant. Drops temperature instantly to freeze locks, metal pipes, or B.O.W. musculature (making them brittle and vulnerable to 1-hit crushing).

## 💉 3. PHARMACEUTICAL INJECTORS & CLINICAL STABILIZERS

* **Epinephrine / Adrenaline Auto-Injector (1/4 Slot — 1 Use):** High-dose adrenaline syrette. Consumed to instantly clear the **Stunned** or **Exhausted** condition and grant +2 to Finesse/Force rolls for 1 round. (Causes 1 Strain backlash when it wears off).
* **Morphine Syrette (1/4 Slot — 1 Use):** Tactical battlefield analgesic. Suppresses physical pain, allowing a survivor to ignore all negative dice penalties from injury or missing Strain boxes for 1 scene.
* **Atropine Antidote Auto-Pen (1/4 Slot — 1 Use):** Clinical nerve agent and bio-toxin antidote. Instantly cures the **Poisoned** condition and cleanses bloodstream toxins.
* **T-Virus Suppressant Injection (UMB-7 Antiviral) (1/4 Slot — 1 Use):** Experimental monoclonal antibody serum developed by Umbrella Pharmaceuticals. Halts the Infection Track for 24 hours or rolls back Infection Stage by -1.

## 🔬 4. LAB GLASSWARE, FIELD TOOLS & SYNTHESIS HARDWARE

* **Botanical Herb Mortar & Pestle (1/4 Slot — ⬜⬜⬜ Durability):** Heavy marble or ceramic grinder. Required to refine raw botanical leaves into fine powder for dual/triple herb synthesis (G+G, G+R, G+B, G+R+B).
* **Graduated Glass Pipette & Beaker Set (1/4 Slot):** Precision measurement glassware for titration, acid mixing, and chemical fluid dilution.
* **Portable Bunsen Burner / Alcohol Torch (1/4 Slot):** Compact brass heating apparatus for distilling reagents, boiling water, or sealing chemical ampoules.
* **Centrifuge & Field Chemical Mixer (1 Slot):** Laboratory device used to separate viral blood samples or synthesize pure antiviral serums from crude reagents.
* **Universal Litmus Indicator Strips (1/4 Slot):** Chemical test strips used to identify hazardous toxins, acidic fluids, or contaminated water supplies.

## ☣️ 5. SPECIALIZED BIO-CHEMICAL AGENTS (UMBRELLA NOMENCLATURE)

* **UMB No. 3 (Chemical Reagent):** Red fluid compound used as a biological catalyst.
* **NP-003 (Neutralizing Agent):** Volatile blue solvent used to break down cellular walls of mutated plant and parasitic B.O.W. growths.
* **VP-017 (Poisonous Toxin):** Green liquid reagent; extremely toxic when vaporized.
* **V-JOLT / Plant Destroyer (1 Slot):** The classic multi-step compounded plant herbicide synthesized from UMB No.3, NP-003, and water. Destroys massive biological vines and plant-based bioweapons instantly.
* **Thermal Phosphorus Powder (1/4 Slot):** Pyrotechnic chemical powder that burns at 2,200°C underwater or in open air. Used to coat bullets or forge incendiary grenades.
