# 👅 LICKER (EVOLVED B.O.W. PREDATOR)

* **Parent Bestiary:** [[B.O.W. & Creature Bestiary]]
* **Threat Tier:** Major / Apex Stalker
* **Armor:** ⬜⬜⬜ (3 Boxes)
* **Perception:** Completely Blind — Hyper-Acoustic Auditory Tracking (+1 Noise triggers immediate 60ft charge).
* **Speed / Distance Bands:** Can leap from Far (20–60ft) to Melee (0–5ft) in a single turn.

---

## 🎯 Threat Statblock & Incoming Attacks

* **Silent Stalking:** Clings to ceiling trusses, steel girders, and high brick facades.
* **Lethal Tongue Strike (Close / Far band, 10–30ft):**
  * Forces **Finesse TN 16** to evade.
  * *Failure:* Inflicts **2 Strain** and applies the **Bleeding** condition.
* **Leaping Scythe Slash (Melee band, 0–5ft):**
  * Forces **Force or Finesse TN 18**.
  * *Failure:* Inflicts **2 Strain**, destroys 1 Armor box, and knocks survivor prone.
* **Decapitating Guillotine (If Survivor is Prone):**
  * Forces **Force TN 20** to deflect with a knife or heavy weapon.
  * *Failure:* Instant incapacitation / lethal trauma.

---

## ⚠️ Tactical Weaknesses & Exploits

1. **Acoustic Manipulation:** If survivors make zero noise (Sneak TN 14) and throw a brick/flare into another zone, the Licker prioritizes the sound source.
2. **Heavy Area Weapons:** Shotgun point-blank blasts (Melee band) deal double damage. Magnum / Grenade Launcher rounds bypass 1 Armor box.
3. **Environmental Hazard:** High-speed impacts (e.g. [[Zone 2.2C - Scripted Event (Runaway Freight Train)]]) pulverize a Licker instantly.
