# 🧟 URBAN SHAMBLER (SOLITARY WANDERER)

* **Parent Bestiary:** [[B.O.W. & Creature Bestiary]]
* **Threat Tier:** Minor (Swarm / Roamer)
* **Armor:** ⬜ (1 Box)
* **Noise Sensitivity:** Drifts toward any sound (+1 Noise brings 1 shambler into adjacent corridors).

---

## Profile & Behavior
A freshly turned civilian or police officer. Sluggish gait, vacant milky eyes, persistent guttural groan. Moves slowly in darkness or open space, but lunges with sudden desperate momentum when within grab reach.

---

## Incoming Threats & Attacks
* **Lunging Grab:** Forces Finesse or Force TN 12. 
  * *Failure:* Inflicts 1 Strain and sets up an immediate bite next turn unless broken.
* **Bite:** If a survivor starts their turn already grabbed, forces Force TN 15 to break free. 
  * *Failure:* Inflicts 1 Strain, causes the **Bleeding** condition, and advances the **Virus Infection Track by +1 Stage**.
  * *(Defensive Counter:* Can be countered instantly by expending 1 Knife Durability box or firing 1 Handgun shot).*

---

## Tactical Traits
* **Strengths:** Ignores non-lethal body trauma; unphased by darkness or fear.
* **Weaknesses:** Headshots (Called Shot TN 18 destroys instantly).
* **V-ACT Risk:** **High**. If corpse is left intact without burning (Kerosene + Lighter) or decapitation, revives as a [[B.O.W. & Creature Bestiary#4-crimson-head-v-act-mutation|Crimson Head]] after 2 scenes.
