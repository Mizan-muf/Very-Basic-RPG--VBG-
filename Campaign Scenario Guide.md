## 3. OPENING SCENARIO: ZERO HOUR (RACCOON PRIME MALL @ J-13)

### Scene 1: The Ambient Cracks (19:45 EST)
* **Location:** Ground Floor Central Fountain Atrium.
* **Atmosphere:** Neon signs humming, rain pounding the skylights, distant sirens echoing across Mission Street.
* **Intercom Advisory 1:** *"Attention Raccoon Prime shoppers. Security reminds all patrons to remain alert. Please report any aggressive, disoriented, or visibly ill individuals to floor staff immediately."*

### Scene 2: The Parking Structure B Standoff
* **Location:** East Parking Structure B exit leading out toward **Ferry Street / East Rail Conduits**.
* **The Incident:** Senior mall security guards chain the roll-up gates as an uncoordinated, violent crowd hammers on the glass outside in the torrential rain.
* **First Aid Encounter:**
  * Pharmacist Arthur Pendelton is wrapping a pressure bandage around rookie guard Officer Kevin Vance’s left shoulder.
  * **Investigation Check (Focus TN 10):** A quick inspection confirms a deep jagged laceration from a broken turnstile pipe—not a bite mark. This builds tension without prematurely triggering infection panic.

### Scene 3: The Scavenge Window (Pre-Lockdown Exploration)
Survivors have approximately 15 minutes before complete closure to acquire starter gear:
* **Grizzly Outdoors (2nd Floor):** Hunting Knives (⬜⬜⬜), Camp Hatchets, Heavy Crowbars (+1 Breaching).
* **Pharm-Plus Express (1st Floor):** Green Herb pots (1/4 slot), basic gauze, antiseptic sprays.
* **Tech Nexus (Electronics Wing):** Bank of CRT displays broadcasting erratic news reports:
  * *Channel 4 News:* Military roadblocks sealing the Arklay perimeter on the northern highways; static interference cutting the broadcast.
* **Mall Maintenance & Security Offices (Basement Level):** Master service keys, fuse boxes, and access hatches to the subterranean drainage runoff conduits.

### Scene 4: Level 4 Quarantine Broadcast (20:15 EST)
* **The Intercom Alarm:** The cheerful mall music cuts violently to the Emergency Broadcast System tone.
* **The Official Order:**
  > *"Emergency Broadcast: By order of the Mayor’s Office and Federal Emergency Management, Raccoon City is now under mandatory Level 4 Quarantine. All citizens are instructed to immediately shelter in place, lock all exterior entrances, and remain indoors until dawn. Municipal emergency services are currently unavailable."*
* **The Lockdown:** Heavy motorized steel security shutters grind down over all public glass entrances, trapping survivors and shoppers inside.

### Branch 1: The North Alley Evacuation Bus
* **Deployment:** Security Chief Raymond Miller coordinates three municipal transit buses departing the North Bay onto **Mission Street (`J-13`)**, attempting to cross **Mission Bridge (`G-13`)** toward the RPD precinct.
* **The Encounter:**
  * A burning tanker and gridlocked civilian vehicles cause a massive pileup at the bridge approach (`G-13`).
  * The crash draws an infected crowd surging from the river alleys.
  * **Tactical Choice:** Break through the barricades on foot toward **City Hall / R.P.D. (`E-13`)**, or divert South into the underground tunnels at the **Subway Station (`J-14`)**.

### Branch 2: Fortifying the Galleria
* **Deployment:** Survivors choose to stay put, barricading the 3rd-floor security suite and retail corridors.
* **The Escalation:**
  * City power grid surges and dies as the **R.C. Substation (`E-15`)** trips. Emergency red backup lights activate.
  * Trapped, infected shoppers in the lower levels begin mutating into active **Urban Shamblers** and early **Crimson Heads**.
* **Escape Vector:** Survivors must navigate the pitch-black mall basement to find the maintenance drainage sluice gate that empties directly into the **Circular River embankments (`G-12`)**.
