## 1. PRIMARY OPERATIONAL THEATER (EAST DISTRICT & THE TRANSIT CORRIDORS)

### Geographic Master Index (Canonical Coordinates)
* **Starting Hub (Mall Complex):** **Raccoon Prime Galleria / Shopping Mall (Building A & Building B)** (`J-13 / J-14`).
* **East Flank (`K-13`):** **Raccoon Theater**, **East Power Substation**, and the **Circular River Embankment**.

### ZONE 1.1: FIRST FLOOR — CENTRAL FOUNTAIN ATRIUM & RETAIL PROMENADE

#### Atmosphere & Narrative Hooks
Flickering halogen fixtures reflect off a marble fountain. Shoppers carry shopping bags while casting nervous glances toward the exits. Muffled emergency sirens wail outside along Mission Street. Every few minutes, the cheerful mall muzak is interrupted by static-heavy PA chimes reminding patrons to report agitated or disoriented individuals.

#### Notable Establishments & Locations to Search

1. **Pharm-Plus Express (Corner Pharmacy & Wellness):**
   * *Environment:* Glass front display with shelves of vitamins, cold medicine, and botanical displays. The pharmacist, Arthur Pendelton, is frantically packing a gym bag behind the prescription counter.
   * *Interactive Points:*
     * *Botanical Display Counter:* Potted **Green Herbs** (decorative medicinal plants).
     * *Locked Prescription Cage:* Heavy steel rolling gate requiring a **Lockpicking (Finesse TN 14)** check or **Crowbar (Force TN 16)**.
   * *Scavenge & Loot Table (1d6 or Search Focus TN 12):*
     * `1–2:` **1x Green Herb (G)** (Consumable, recovers 1 Strain / cures Bleeding).
     * `3–4:` **Green Herb (G)** (Restores 2 Strain, cures Bleeding).
     * `5:` **Pharmaceutical Syringe & Saline** (Can be combined with herbs to create injectable doses).
     * `6 (Locked Cage):` **1x First Aid Spray Bottle** (Restores all Strain, removes Bleeding & Limb Damaged).

2. **King’s Cutlery & Kitchenware:**
   * *Environment:* Gourmet lifestyle store with wooden cutting blocks and display cases of chef knives and cast-iron cookware.
   * *Interactive Points:*
     * *Display Knife Block:* Glass case holding high-carbon steel chef knives.
     * *Camping Stove Counter:* Propane canisters and butane torch lighters.
   * *Scavenge & Loot Table:*
     * `1–3:` **Heavy Chef Knife / Meat Cleaver** (Melee, ⬜⬜⬜ Durability, Reach: Melee).
     * `4–5:` **Butane Torch & Fuel Canister** (Light source, can ignite gasoline or oil slicks).
     * `6:` **Reinforced Apron & Leather Cooking Gloves** (Grants ⬜ Armor box against scratches/glass cuts).

3. **Food Court & Back Kitchen Service Alley:**
   * *Environment:* Smells of stale fryer oil and burnt coffee. Half-eaten burgers sit abandoned on plastic trays. The rear kitchen service corridor connects directly to the freight elevators.
   * *Interactive Points:*
     * *Walk-in Meat Locker:* Heavy insulated door. A shivering mall janitor is hiding inside.
     * *Commercial Gas Line:* A wrench can loosen the brass valve to flood the corridor with flammable natural gas.
   * *Scavenge & Loot Table:*
     * `1–2:` **Heavy Cast-Iron Skillet** (Blunt melee, ⬜⬜ Durability, Stagger on Crit).
     * `3–4:` **Box of Matches & Cooking Oil Jug** (Can create a 1-Scene fire barricade).
     * `5–6:` **Janitor’s Service Ring (Key A)** (Opens Food Court maintenance doors and Basement stairwells).

### ZONE 1.2: SECOND FLOOR — MEZZANINE & OUTDOOR OUTFITTERS

#### Atmosphere & Narrative Hooks
The second-floor overlook offers a vantage point of the entire atrium. Store shutters are halfway down. An electronics store glows in the shadows with wall-to-wall television displays broadcasting flickering static and emergency crawls.

#### Notable Establishments & Locations to Search

1. **Grizzly Outdoors & Tactical Supply:**
   * *Environment:* Wood-paneled store decorated with hunting trophies, camping tents, and tactical apparel. The cash register is smashed open; the owner fled earlier with the hunting rifles, leaving behind utility and camping equipment.
   * *Interactive Points:*
     * *Archery & Bow Display:* Glass wall case containing compound hunting bows and field-point arrows.
     * *Heavy Tool Rack:* Sturdy fire axes, crowbars, and survival machetes secured with a steel cable lock (**Force TN 14** to snap or **Bolt Cutters**).
   * *Scavenge & Loot Table:*
     * `1–2:` **Survival Hunting Knife** (Melee, ⬜⬜⬜ Durability, +1 to Stealth takedowns).
     * `3–4:` **Heavy Demolition Crowbar** (Melee, ⬜⬜⬜ Durability, +2 bonus to breaching doors/crates).
     * `5:` **Tactical Crossbow + 6 Bolts** (Ranged, Silent, Noise 0, Reach: Close/Far, requires Minor action to reload).
     * `6:` **Kevlar Hunting Vest** (Body Armor: ⬜⬜ [2 Boxes], absorbs 2 hits before shredding permanently).

2. **Tech Nexus & Sound System Hub:**
   * *Environment:* Electronics boutique. Over three dozen CRT televisions are tuned to Channel 4 News and Raccoon City Local Broadcasting.
   * *Interactive Points:*
     * *Broadcast Bank:* Live feeds show military roadblocks with sandbags and razor wire sealing the Arklay highway exits (`A-1` to `E-1`) before the camera feed cuts abruptly to color bars and tone.
     * *PA Amplifier Console:* A microphone station tied directly into the mall’s intercom system.
   * *Scavenge & Loot Table:*
     * `1–3:` **Heavy-Duty Tactical Flashlight + D-Batteries** (Illuminates pitch-black rooms, negates darkness penalties).
     * `4–5:` **Handheld Shortwave Transceiver (Walkie-Talkie)** (Allows communication across different sectors).
     * `6:` **High-Capacity Battery Pack & Wire Strippers** (Can hotwire electrical bypass boxes).

3. **Raccoon Cineplex Mezzanine (Screenings 1–6):**
   * *Environment:* Dark carpeted cinema corridor lit only by glowing red EXIT signs. Muffled groaning and wet tearing sounds echo from Screening Room 3 (The first active Shambler feeding encounter if survivors investigate).
   * *Interactive Points:*
     * *Screening Room 3 Encounter:* An infected cinema projectionist is crouched over a fallen usher. If startled by bright lights or loud footsteps, it lunges into Melee reach.
     * *Concession Storage Room:* Locked with a numeric keypad (**Focus TN 14** or find code in Management Office).
   * *Scavenge & Loot Table:*
     * `1–3:` **Brass Velvet Rope Stanchion** (Heavy blunt weapon, ⬜⬜ Durability, Melee reach).
     * `4–5:` **Box of Flares (x3)** (Burns bright red for 1 Scene, repels infected dogs/animals, Noise +1).
     * `6 (Concession Safe):` **Box of 9mm Handgun Rounds (x15)** (Left behind by an off-duty security guard).

### ZONE 1.3: THIRD FLOOR — SECURITY OPERATIONS & EXECUTIVE SUITE (SAFE ROOM)

#### Atmosphere & Narrative Hooks
A restricted administrative corridor with gray soundproofed doors, carpeted executive offices, and the primary Mall Security Dispatch Suite. A glowing green EXIT sign buzzes steadily. Soft typewriter music can be heard in the safe room zone.

#### Notable Establishments & Locations to Search

1. **Security Dispatch Control Room (THE SAFE ROOM):**
   * *Environment:* The room is fortified with a heavy steel security door, reinforced deadbolts, and a desk with a desktop lamp and an old manual typewriter.
   * *Features & Interactive Points:*
     * **Safe Room Sanctuary:** No infected will enter this room as long as the heavy deadbolt is engaged.
     * **CCTV Monitor Wall:** 8 operational CRT monitors displaying live camera feeds across the Mall Atrium, Parking Gate B, 2nd Floor Mezzanine, and the East Loading Dock (`K-13`).
     * **Typewriter & Ink Ribbon:** Survivors can record notes, file dossier updates, and recover 1 Strain through a Moment of Respite.
     * **Gun Locker (Locked — Master Key Required):** Heavy steel gun safe labeled *R.P.D. Auxiliary Security*.
   * *Loot & Equipment:*
     * **Security Master Keycard (Blue Level):** Opens all second-floor service doors and elevator shafts.
     * **Box of 9mm Ammo (x10 rounds)**.
     * **1x Green Herb (G)** sitting in a porcelain mug on the desk.
     * **Security Gun Locker (If unlocked via Keycard + Code 0928):**
       * **Pump-Action 12-Gauge Shotgun** (⬜⬜⬜ Durability, Capacity: 6 shells, Noise Profile: Loud 6/6, Point-blank spread).
       * **Box of 12-Gauge Shotgun Shells (x6)**.

2. **Mall Manager’s Office & Records Vault:**
   * *Environment:* Mahogany desk, overturned leather swivel chair, and shattered glass windows overlooking the atrium.
   * *Interactive Points:*
     * *Office Safe (Dial Combination):* Memo on the desk reads: *"Remember to change the gate code to the date of the downtown fireworks display (09-28)."*
     * *Floor Schematics & Blueprints:* Detailed architectural blueprints of the Mall, Basement HVAC tunnels, and the drainage conduit connection to the **Circular River (`K-13`)**.
   * *Scavenge & Loot Table:*
     * **Mall Sub-Basement Map & Conduit Key** (Reveals the secret sewer bypass in Option B).
     * **Heavy Brass Desk Clock** (Thrown distraction tool, creates Noise in an adjacent room).

### ZONE 1.4: BASEMENT LEVEL & EAST LOADING BAY (`K-13`)

#### Atmosphere & Narrative Hooks
Cold concrete walls, exposed overhead water pipes dripping with condensation, and the low hum of industrial ventilation shafts. Smells of diesel exhaust and damp earth.

#### Notable Establishments & Locations to Search

1. **Parking Structure B Exit Gate (Scene 2 Narrative Event):**
   * *The Incident:* Senior security officers are chaining the industrial roll-up shutter as an aggressive crowd batters the glass.
   * *NPCs:* Officer Kevin Vance (wounded shoulder) and Arthur Pendelton (administering crushed green herbs).
   * *Interactable:* Emergency gate manual winch crank, fire extinguisher cabinet.
   * *Loot:* **Wall-Mounted Fire Axe** (Melee, ⬜⬜⬜ Durability, +1 Force to chopping obstacles).

2. **Sub-Basement Boiler & HVAC Generator Vault:**
   * *Environment:* Massive industrial boilers, steel catwalks, and fuse breaker boxes powering the mall's grid connection to the **East Power Substation (`K-13`)**.
   * *Hazards:* Leaking high-pressure steam pipes (**Finesse TN 12** to navigate without suffering 1 Strain).
   * *Scavenge & Loot Table:*
     * **Heavy Pipe Wrench** (Melee, ⬜⬜ Durability).
     * **2x Spare Industrial Fuses (15A)** (Used to restore power to elevator shafts or safe room security doors).
     * **Can of WD-40 Lubricant** (Silences squeaking doors or unjams rusted machinery).

3. **North Loading Bay (Branch A Staging Ground):**
   * *Environment:* Large open garage with three idling **Blue Municipal Transit Buses (#12, #14, #18)** surrounded by exhaust fumes. Security Chief Raymond Miller is directing civilians aboard.
   * *Interactable:* Bus engine bays, spare tire racks, driver log clipboards.
   * *Loot:* **Tire Iron** (Blunt weapon), **First Aid Box on Bus #14** (Contains 1x Green Herb).

4. **East Service Backdoor (`K-13` Backdoor to Option B):**
   * *Environment:* Heavy steel fire door leading into the dark alleyway between the **Raccoon Theater** and the **East Power Substation**.
   * *Interactable:* Push-bar exit (One-way exterior lock; once pushed, the door locks behind the party).

### OPTION A: THE NORTH/WEST BUS CONVOY (Street Survival)
1. **The Transit Route:** Bus #14 departs North Loading Bay onto Mission Street (`J-13`), heading West and North around the rear perimeter of the R.P.D. toward **Raccoon City Hall (`I-12 / I-13`)**.
2. **The In-Cabin Mutation:** A wounded passenger in the rear seats succumbs to the T-Virus, mutating into an active **Urban Shambler**. Screams erupt in the confined space.
3. **The Collision:** The driver swerves in panic, crashing the bus through an intersection barricade at **Grid Sector I-12 / I-13**.
4. **The Aftermath (Noise Clock 5/6):** The crash ruptures the fuel tank, igniting an inferno that draws an ambient horde from the surrounding alleys. Survivors must escape into:
   * **Vector A1:** The grand portico doors of **Raccoon City Hall (`I-12`)**.
   * **Vector A2:** The maintenance loading bay of **Central Station (`H-12`)**.

### OPTION B: FORTIFYING THE MALL & THE EAST RIVER RUN (Dungeon Crawl)
1. **The Grid Outage:** The **East Power Substation (`K-13`)** is swarmed and fails. The mall plunges into pitch darkness broken only by emergency red strobes.
2. **The Indoor Outbreak:** Unchecked infected shoppers throughout the Atrium rise as Shamblers and Crimson Heads.
3. **The Backdoor Escape:** Survivors use the **East Service Backdoor (`K-13`)** to exit behind the **Raccoon Theater**.
4. **The Convergence:** Survivors can either search the **Circular River Embankment (`K-13`)** for drainage barges or push West toward the smoking crash site at **City Hall (`I-12 / I-13`)** to rendezvous with the remnants of the convoy.
