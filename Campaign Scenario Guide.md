# BIOHAZARD: CAMPAIGN MASTER SCENARIO GUIDE
*A Living VBG-Z Campaign Module & Geographic Sourcebook for Raccoon City.*

---

## 1. PRIMARY OPERATIONAL THEATER (EAST DISTRICT & THE TRANSIT CORRIDORS)

### Starting Sector: Raccoon City Mall Complex (Grid J-13 & J-14)
* **Primary Facility:** **Raccoon City Mall (Building A & Building B)** (`J-13 / J-14`).
* **Immediate Neighboring Landmarks (East Sector Geography):**
  * **East of Mall (`K-13`):** **Raccoon Theater** and the **Circular River Embankment**.
  * **North of Mall (`J-12`):** **District Post Office** along the commercial connector.
  * **South of Mall (`J-14`):** **Mall Building B** and the **Multi-Level Parking Garage Structure**.
  * **East of Parking Garage (`K-14 / K-13`):** **Raccoon City Power Substation** (Bordering the East River).
  * **West of Mall (`H-13 / I-13`):** **Raccoon City Hall** and the **Central Station Rail Complex**.
  * **Southwest / Far West (`E-13`):** **Raccoon City Police Department (R.P.D.)** (We operate around its rear approaches; front gates remain the canonical entry point).

## 2. SCENARIO ACT I: ZERO HOUR & THE INITIAL FORK (MALL @ J-13)

### Scene 1: Pre-Lockdown & The Ambient Tension (19:45 EST)
* **Location:** Ground Floor Central Fountain Atrium (Building A).
* **Atmosphere:** Distant sirens, flickering neon lights, mall PA broadcasts warning citizens to stay vigilant and report agitated or symptomatic patrons.
* **First Aid Encounter (Parking Gate B):**
  * Senior security guards chain the roll-up exits against an aggressive mob pounding on the glass outside in the rain.
  * Officer Kevin Vance is being bandaged by Pharmacist Arthur Pendelton. A Focus TN 10 check confirms it is a jagged metal turnstile laceration (not an infection bite), providing temporary relief while ratcheting up tension.

### Scene 2: The Mandatory Quarantine Alert (20:15 EST)
* **The Citywide Siren:** PA chime cuts to the Emergency Broadcast tone.
* **The Announcement:**
  > *"Emergency Broadcast: By order of the Mayor’s Office, Raccoon City is under mandatory Level 4 Quarantine due to violent rioters and rabid individuals. Citizens are instructed to shelter in place until morning. Exterior doors will be locked."*
* **Lockdown:** Heavy hydraulic steel shutters slam down over all main mall entrances, sealing survivors inside.

### OPTION A: THE NORTH/WEST BUS CONVOY (Street-Level Survival)

1. **The Route & Objective:**
   * Security Chief Raymond Miller announces municipal evacuation buses waiting in the North Loading Bay.
   * **Route:** Instead of heading South toward the front R.P.D. gates, the convoy heads **North and West** around the rear perimeter of the R.P.D. toward **Raccoon City Hall (`I-12 / I-13`)**.

2. **The In-Transit Mutiny & Crash:**
   * Several passengers onboard were already harboring latent T-Virus infection. Halfway through the route, they turn into aggressive **Urban Shamblers** inside the passenger cabin.
   * Panic erupts. The driver loses control and slams the bus into an intersection barricade at **Grid Sector I-12 / I-13** (Right outside City Hall and Central Station).

3. **The Immediate Intersection Crisis:**
   * The driver kicks the emergency doors open. Screaming civilians scatter in all directions.
   * The loud crash and engine fire tick the **Area Noise Clock directly to 5/6**, drawing dozens of shamblers from the adjacent alleys.
   * **Tactical Escape Vectors for Survivors:**
     * **Vector 1:** Breach the main doors of **Raccoon City Hall (`I-12`)** to seek administrative sanctuary.
     * **Vector 2:** Sprint across the plaza and force open the **Rear Emergency Exits of Central Station (`H-12`)** to get into the train concourse and rail lines.

---

### OPTION B: FORTIFYING THE MALL & THE EAST RIVER RUN (Dungeon Crawl)

1. **The Lockdown & Collapse:**
   * Survivors choose to remain inside the mall, securing the 2nd-floor corridors and service access.
   * The **Raccoon Power Substation (`K-13 / K-14`)** just east of the mall is overrun by shamblers and fails, cutting primary electricity to the entire mall complex. Red emergency backup strobes activate.

2. **The Sub-Basement & Backdoor Escape (`K-13`):**
   * Trapped civilians inside the dark retail wings succumb to airborne/waterborne viral strains and begin roaming the corridors.
   * Survivors locate the mall’s **East Service Backdoor (`K-13`)**, exiting out behind the mall complex right between the **Raccoon Theater** and the **Power Substation**.

3. **The Circular River Conduits & City Hall Convergence:**
   * Outside in the rain, the Power Substation is completely surrounded by shambler clusters.
   * Survivors have two tactical choices:
     * **Vector 1 (The River Vector):** Push directly East toward the **Circular River embankment (`K-13`)** to search for drainage canals, maintenance barges, or water conduits leading out of the city.
     * **Vector 2 (The City Hall Flank):** Push West along the service road toward the **Raccoon City Hall plaza (`I-12 / I-13`)**.
     * *The Convergence:* Arriving at City Hall, survivors witness the smoking wreckage of the Option A Bus Convoy, now completely swarmed by hordes drawn by the crash. Survivors can scavenge dead convoy supplies while dealing with the massive ambient horde.
